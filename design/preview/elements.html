Miku elements — beyond the palette

Candidate motifs to make the Miku/飴 identity felt across the app. Each tagged by licensing risk for a public repo.

Licensing reality: Hatsune Miku's character art / likeness / name-as-branding is owned by Crypton Future Media (Piapro Character License — non-commercial, conditional). Safe forever: her signature color, abstract candy/ribbon motifs, and fan-culture food memes (leek/negi) & the "39" pun. Avoid: her actual face/twin-tail character render, or naming the mascot "Miku".

Character render / avatar

🚫👧

Drawing Miku herself (face, canonical outfit, twin-tails as a person) as app art. This is the line — needs a Crypton/Piapro license for anything public or commercial.

avoid · likeness = license

Recommendation: adopt the safe tier freely (leek loader, candy swirl, ribbon divider, hair-flow headers, original candy mascot, 39/3-9 easter eggs). Treat sound as opt-in with original audio. Keep the actual Miku character out of shipped art unless you license it. The result reads unmistakably "Miku-inspired" while staying an ownable, publishable brand.
